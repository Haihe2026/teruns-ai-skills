# Marketplace image rules

Checked October 2026. Rules change: when a rule below matters for a decision, say which
source it comes from. "Official" = the marketplace's own help page. "Reported" = repeated
by seller guides but not confirmed on an official page; treat as a recommendation.

## Amazon (all Amazon stores)

Official technical requirements (Seller Central, Product image requirements):

- 500 to 10,000 px on the longest side.
- JPEG, TIFF, PNG or non-animated GIF. JPEG preferred.
- Larger than 1,000 px on the longest side is preferred so zoom works.
- At least one image per product; Amazon recommends at least six images and one video.

Main image (first image, shown in search results) — Amazon's main image standard,
as widely documented by sellers; confirm details in Seller Central help page G1881:

- Pure white background, RGB 255, 255, 255.
- The product fills about 85% of the frame.
- Shows only the product being sold: no props or accessories that are not included.
- No text, logos (other than on the product itself), watermarks, borders, colour blocks,
  badges or inset images.
- A photo or realistic render of the actual product, not a drawing. Not inside its
  packaging unless the packaging is an important feature.
- Whole product in frame, sharp, evenly lit, realistic colour.

Additional images (slots 2–9): lifestyle, infographics with text, close-ups, size views,
comparisons and package contents are allowed. Same product, same colour, no misleading
claims. Text must be accurate and readable on a phone.

Recommended working size: 2,000 × 2,000 px (2K square). 4K is accepted (under 10,000 px).

## Amazon A+ content

Module image sizes (seller guides; confirm in A+ Content Manager before final export):

| Module | Image size (px) |
| --- | --- |
| Image header with text | 970 × 600 |
| Image with text overlay | 970 × 300 |
| Three images and text | 300 × 300 each |
| Four images and text | 220 × 220 each |
| Single image and sidebar / highlights | 300 × 300 |
| Company logo | 600 × 180 |
| Premium A+ full-width modules | up to 1,464 px wide |

Reported limits: JPG or PNG, RGB, under 2 MB per image, no animation. Keep important
text out of the image where a module offers a text field (better on mobile and for
search); when text is in the image, keep it large and short.

## Shopify

Official (Shopify Help Center, product media types):

- Up to 5,000 × 5,000 px or 25 megapixels; file under 20 MB.
- 2,048 × 2,048 px recommended for square images.
- PNG preferred, then JPEG; also TIFF, BMP, GIF, SVG, HEIC, WebP.
- Use one consistent aspect ratio across a store's featured images.

## Etsy

Official (Etsy Help, Requirements and Best Practices for Images):

- First photo at least 635 × 635 px; at least 2,000 × 2,000 px recommended.
- Keep each file under 1 MB (larger uploads may fail).
- JPG, GIF, PNG only. No animated GIFs; transparent areas show as black.
- sRGB colour profile (not CMYK).
- First photo horizontal or square; later photos should match its shape.

## TikTok Shop

Reported (seller guides; confirm in TikTok Shop Seller Center):

- Main image at least 600 × 600 px; 800 × 800 px or more recommended.
- Up to 9 images per listing; 5 or more good images help listing quality ratings.
- Clean white or light background for the main image; no watermarks, borders,
  promotional text or collages.
- AI-generated product scenes or models may need an "AI-generated" disclosure.

## AI disclosure (all marketplaces)

Generated images must show the real product truthfully: same shape, colour, materials,
printed text and included items. From August 2026 the EU AI Act adds labelling duties
for AI-generated images; sellers in EU stores should keep the AI-generated note that
Teruns writes into each file's metadata and follow their marketplace's disclosure rules.
